### Beginner Challenges (Levels 1–25)
*Focus: Basic piece movements, simple captures, and one-move checkmates.*
 * **Challenge 1:** Mate in 1 (Back-Rank) — Deliver checkmate using a rook or queen on the opponent's back rank. **Win Condition:** Force checkmate in 1 move.
 * **Challenge 2:** Mate in 1 (Scholar's Mate pattern) — Exploit an unprotected f7 pawn. **Win Condition:** Deliver checkmate targeting f7.
 * **Challenge 3:** The Simple Fork — Attack two pieces simultaneously with a knight. **Win Condition:** Win the higher-value piece on the next turn.
 * **Challenge 4:** Winning a Free Piece — Capitalize on an undefended minor piece. **Win Condition:** Capture the hanging piece without compensation.
 * **Challenge 5:** Back-Rank Weakness — Exploit a lack of luft (pawn escape square) for the enemy king. **Win Condition:** Checkmate the king on the 8th (or 1st) rank.
 * **Challenge 6:** Simple Pin — Immobilize an enemy piece that shields a more valuable piece. **Win Condition:** Capture the pinned piece after breaking support.
 * **Challenge 7:** Simple Skewer — Attack a high-value piece, forcing it to move and exposing a piece behind it. **Win Condition:** Win the backend piece.
 * **Challenge 8:** Removing the Defender — Capture or deflect the sole piece guarding a key target. **Win Condition:** Capture the newly undefended target on the next move.
 * **Challenge 9:** Simple Double Attack — Threaten two separate objectives at once with a queen or rook. **Win Condition:** Secure material advantage by taking one of the targets.
 * **Challenge 10:** Discovered Attack — Move a piece out of the way to unveil a direct threat from a rook, bishop, or queen. **Win Condition:** Win material from the secondary threat.
 * **Challenge 11:** Mate in 2 (Smothered Mate setup) — Trap a king completely surrounded by its own pieces using a knight check. **Win Condition:** Deliver checkmate with a knight.
 * **Challenge 12:** Pawn Promotion Race — Calculate the exact tempo needed to queen a passed pawn before the opponent can stop it. **Win Condition:** Successfully promote the pawn and convert the resulting endgame.
 * **Challenge 13:** Trapped Piece — Cut off all flight squares of an enemy bishop or knight. **Win Condition:** Capture the trapped piece on the subsequent move.
 * **Challenge 14:** Overloaded Defender — Force a single piece to defend two critical threats simultaneously. **Win Condition:** Capture the piece that is abandoned by the over-tasked defender.
 * **Challenge 15:** Simple X-Ray Attack — Attack through an enemy piece to target a square or piece behind it. **Win Condition:** Win material or achieve a positional breakthrough.
 * **Challenge 16:** Intermediate Fork (Queen) — Utilize diagonal and orthogonal ranges to fork a king and rook. **Win Condition:** Win the exchange or rook.
 * **Challenge 17:** Defensive Interposition — Block an incoming check or dangerous line of sight with a tactical sacrifice. **Win Condition:** Neutralize the attack and stabilize the position.
 * **Challenge 18:** Simple Clearance Sacrifice — Sacrifice a piece to clear a crucial square or file for an ally. **Win Condition:** Follow up with a winning tactical blow.
 * **Challenge 19:** Breaking the King's Pawn Shield — Exchange pieces to strip away protective pawns around the castled king. **Win Condition:** Open lines for a subsequent mating attack.
 * **Challenge 20:** Basic Deflection — Force an enemy piece away from a defensive post using a sudden threat. **Win Condition:** Exploit the vacant square.
 * **Challenge 21:** Interception — Cut the communication line between two cooperating enemy pieces. **Win Condition:** Win material due to broken coordination.
 * **Challenge 22:** Seventh-Rank Rook Incursion — Invade the opponent's seventh rank to harvest pawns. **Win Condition:** Win at least two pawns and create a mating net.
 * **Challenge 23:** Centralizing the Knight — Maneuvering a knight to an outpost where it controls key squares. **Win Condition:** Restrict enemy movement and win material.
 * **Challenge 24:** Simple Counter-Check — Respond to a check with an even stronger intermediate check. **Win Condition:** Turn the initiative and win the attacking piece.
 * **Challenge 25:** Opposition Basics — Use the king to out-flank the opposing king in a fundamental pawn endgame. **Win Condition:** Promote a pawn to win the game.
