In Challenges mode, Chess contains two sets with 15 levels each:

1. **Missing Piece** — White starts with at least one missing piece, loses more
   material as the levels increase, and can take as many moves as needed to
   finish by checkmate or stalemate.
2. **Limited Moves** — Put the black king in check or checkmate within the
   level's allowed move limit. The limit increases by level and never exceeds
   25 moves.

Both sets start with White to move.
Hints only highlight a recommended next move on the board; they no longer show
the old tactical descriptions.
