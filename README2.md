In Challenges mode, Chess contains three groups of ten custom positions:

1. **Scholar's Mate Challenge** — White to move; checkmate in 39 moves or fewer.
2. **No Queen Challenge** — Start without the white queen; win without using a queen, including on promotion.
3. **Fast Checkmate Challenge** — White to move; checkmate in 25 moves or fewer.
4. **Pawn Promotion Challenge** — Promote the a7 pawn, then win.
5. **Castle & Conquer Challenge** — Castle kingside, then win.
6. **Bishop Pair Challenge** — Win while keeping both white bishops alive.
7. **Rook Checkmate Challenge** — Deliver the final checkmate with a rook.
8. **Material Comeback Challenge** — Win from a starting deficit of at least five points.
9. **One-Piece Army Challenge** — Only knights may make captures; then win.
10. **Knight Hunter Challenge** — Capture both black knights before any other black piece, then win in 25 moves or fewer.

The first group is **Beginner**, followed by **Getting Started** and **Easy** groups.
Each challenge has its own board setup and starts with White to move.
Hints only highlight a recommended next move on the board; they no longer show
the old tactical descriptions.
